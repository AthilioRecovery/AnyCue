# Code of Conduct

AnyCue is a space for building software together. We want it to be welcoming and respectful for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, spam or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks these rules.

To report a problem, email **[eu@athiliodev.com.br](mailto:eu@athiliodev.com.br)**. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions.
